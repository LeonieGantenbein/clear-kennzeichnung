## Overview

CLEAR is a proposal for voluntarily disclosing in what respects and to what extent an artificial intelligence was involved in a text – and who stands behind it. Instead of a blanket yes or no (“AI-generated”), CLEAR provides five statements for each person involved, comparable to a citation: as information about the craft.

> **Culture first, then labelling.**<br>
> A label alone is not enough. As long as disclosure is penalised, only those who can afford it will label, and silence remains the rational choice. CLEAR presupposes a cultural negotiation that has not yet taken place. → Chapter [“Culture before Labelling”](CULTURE.md)

## Quick reference

| Axis | Meaning | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| **C** – Conception | Conception, line of thought | C0 purely human · C1 AI as discussion partner · C2 individual content parts by AI · C3 content comprehensively generated by AI |
| **L** – Language | Linguistic editing | L0 without AI · L1 proofreading · L2 stylistic editing · L3 substantive editing (structure, argument) · L4 translation |
| **E** – Evidence | Sources on which the text rests | E0 without AI · E1 source access · E2 source processing · E3 source interpretation · E4 interpretation of results; suffix (p) published / (n) unpublished |
| **A** – Attribution | Declaration | AI models used and time period |
| **R** – Responsibility | Accountability | R1 fully checked and taken responsibility for · R2 spot-checked · R3 unchecked, no one responsible |

Only the **highest level reached** is given for each axis. Each person involved (author, editor, translator …) gets a line of their own. The label appears before or after the text.

**Example:**

```
CLEAR (author): C1, L3, E(p)1, A: Claude Opus 4.8, Fable 5, Opus 5, 04–09/2026, R1
```

Full description of all levels, guidance on classification and use in educational institutions: chapter [“Specification”](SPECIFICATION.md)

## Status

CLEAR has not yet been tested or evaluated in practice and is certainly incomplete. Feedback, experience from use and proposals for images, video and code are welcome – via [issues in this repository](https://github.com/LeonieGantenbein/clear-kennzeichnung/issues).

## Origin

CLEAR comes from the book [*Zwischen Prompt und Welt. Über das Leben mit KI*](https://www.schwabe.ch/produkt/leonie-gantenbein-zwischen-prompt-und-welt-9783796556333-t-20562) [Between Prompt and World: On Living with AI] by Leonie Gantenbein (forthcoming December 2026, Schwabe Verlag) and builds on concepts from [*Annetzung: Handeln durch Technologie und Imagination*](https://doi.org/10.24894/978-3-7965-5360-8) [Acting through Technology and Imagination] (2025), in particular the concept of world transparency (*Welttransparenz*).
