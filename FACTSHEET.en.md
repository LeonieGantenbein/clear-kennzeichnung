# Factsheet: CLEAR at a glance

The factsheet summarises all abbreviations and rules on one page. You can use it for reference or give it to a language model so that it proposes its involvement in a text according to CLEAR.

## Notation

```
CLEAR (role): C?, L?, E(p|n)?, A: models, period, R?
```

- The highest level reached is given for each axis; it includes the levels below.
- Each person involved gets a line of their own.
- The label appears before or after the text.

## Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **C** | **Conception – conception and line of thought** |
| C0 | The line of thought was developed exclusively by humans. |
| C1 | The AI served as a discussion partner during conception. |
| C2 | Individual content parts were worked out by the AI. |
| C3 | The content was comprehensively generated by the AI. |
| **L** | **Language – linguistic editing** |
| L0 | The language was formulated without AI. |
| L1 | Proofreading: spelling, grammar and punctuation. |
| L2 | Stylistic editing: wording improved, content unchanged. |
| L3 | Substantive editing: interventions in structure and argument. |
| L4 | Translation by the AI. |
| **E** | **Evidence – sources and how they were processed** |
| E0 | Source access and processing without AI. |
| E1 | Source access: the AI finds, obtains or translates sources; the human reads and interprets them. |
| E2 | Source processing: the AI summarises sources; work continues with the summaries. |
| E3 | Source interpretation: the AI’s interpretation of the sources was adopted. |
| E4 | Interpretation of results: a conclusion drawn by the AI appears in the text as a result. |
| (p) | Published material that readers can verify. |
| (n) | Own, unpublished material. |
| **A** | **Attribution – declaration:** AI models used and period of use. |
| **R** | **Responsibility – accountability** |
| R1 | Fully checked and taken responsibility for by the human. |
| R2 | Spot-checked and therefore not fully taken responsibility for. |
| R3 | Published unchecked, with the risk of having to take responsibility for the text nonetheless. |

## Prompt for a language model’s self-declaration

A language model can propose its own involvement. To do so, copy the following text into the same chat at the end of a collaboration. The model then proposes a level for C, L, E and A and gives reasons; R is always set by you.

```
Please label, according to CLEAR, how you were involved in this text. Base your answer exclusively on our conversation so far.

For each axis, give the highest level reached and justify it in one sentence:

C – Conception: C0 line of thought exclusively human · C1 AI as a discussion partner · C2 individual content parts worked out by the AI · C3 content comprehensively generated by the AI
L – Language: L0 without AI · L1 proofreading · L2 stylistic editing (wording, content unchanged) · L3 substantive editing (structure and argument) · L4 translation
E – Evidence: E0 without AI · E1 source access (finding, obtaining, translating) · E2 source processing (summaries replace the primary source) · E3 source interpretation adopted · E4 the AI’s conclusion adopted as a result; suffix (p) published, (n) own unpublished material
A – Attribution: your model name and the period of our collaboration
R – Responsibility: Leave R open. Only the human can make this statement.

Format: CLEAR (role): C?, L?, E(p|n)?, A: model, period, R?
For L, the deepest involvement counts, not the most frequent. If you cannot determine a level with certainty from the conversation, say so instead of guessing.
```

## Limits of the self-declaration

A model can only assess what it has seen. Its statement is therefore only meaningful if the entire work took place with a single language model in a single chat.

If you used several chats, tools or models, revised the text outside the chat, or if the conversation is so long that the model no longer takes its beginnings into account, it cannot fully assess its involvement. In that case, make the classification yourself.

Even in the best case, the model’s statement remains a proposal that you check and correct where necessary. Only a human can take on responsibility (R).
